Who she is

GIUMS (Giu)

Visual artist and illustrator focused on character design for over 15 years, working both in Brazil and internationally, with a strong influence from geek culture, high fantasy, TCG and, above all, RPG.

She is currently building a community on Patreon while developing her original project YATHRA, a high fantasy RPG setting embracing queer culture (LGBTQIAPN+) and dragons, lots of dragons!

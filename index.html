Who she is

GIUMS (Giu)

Visual artist and illustrator focused on character design for over 15 years, working both in Brazil and internationally, with a strong influence from geek culture, high fantasy, TCG and, above all, RPG.

She is currently building a community on Patreon while developing her original project YATHRA: a concept art book and high fantasy swashbuckling RPG setting book, embracing queer culture (LGBTQIAPN+) and dragons. Lots of dragons!

Tabletop RPG & games
